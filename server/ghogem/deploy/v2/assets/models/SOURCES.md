# Model creator marks

These local SVGs are creator marks used to identify the company behind a model. They are not individual model portraits and do not imply endorsement.

Brand icons are from `simple-icons@16.10.0` and were downloaded to this site instead of being hotlinked:

- Mistral AI — `mistralai.svg`, source: https://chat.mistral.ai
- Alibaba Cloud / Qwen — `alibabacloud.svg`, source: https://www.alibabagroup.com/en/ir/reports
- Google — `google.svg`, source: https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com; brand elements: https://about.google/brand-resource-center/brand-elements/
- Meta — `meta.svg`, source: https://www.meta.com; brand guidance: https://www.facebook.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand
- MiniMax — `minimax.svg`, source: https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/MiniMax-01/blob/57cf223b177e99636c7711a0f179e9fdc9c38e8a/figures/minimax.svg; the Simple Icons inclusion records permission from MiniMax in https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/pull/13982
- NVIDIA — `nvidia.svg`, source: https://www.nvidia.com/en-us
- OpenRouter — `openrouter.svg`, source: https://openrouter.ai

Other creators use simple local monogram SVGs. Check the [Simple Icons legal disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md) and each brand's current guidelines before republishing these marks in another context.
